About the school

RKris Academy is in Awadhpur, Gorakhpur, near Motiram Adda, the railway crossing and Pani Tanki. The school offers classes from Nursery to Class VIII.

Principal Ms. Pratima Jaiswal leads the school. The office phone and WhatsApp number is +91 80902 54020. Email: academyrkris@gmail.com.
