Teaching methodology

Experiential learning

Labs, models and field tasks so ideas are practised, not only copied.

Classroom teaching

Lessons planned for the class in front of the teacher, with time to ask questions.

Project-based learning

Term projects that connect subjects and end in a student exhibition.

Doubt-clearing sessions

Weekly slots to ask, retry and leave the topic clearer than they found it.

Collaborative work

Pair and group tasks that build explanation skills and fairness.

Values in the timetable

Assemblies and circle time that treat courtesy as a practised habit.
