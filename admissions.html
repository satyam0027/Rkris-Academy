How to apply

Admission process

1

Register

Submit the enquiry form or collect a form from the school office.

2

Documents

Share birth proof, photographs and previous-school records as listed below.

3

Interaction

Age-appropriate observation (early years) or interaction (higher classes) by appointment.

4

Offer

Selected families receive an offer letter, usually within seven working days.

5

Fee confirmation

Pay the admission fee within the stated window to reserve the seat.

6

Orientation

Attend the April orientation and collect books, uniform guidance and transport options.

  • Previous school report card / transfer certificate (Class II upwards)
