Programme

Curriculum

Pre-Primary

Play-based language and number readiness, movement, art and gentle routines that make school feel safe.

Primary

Strong foundations in English, Hindi, mathematics and EVS, with reading time, clubs and activity periods.

Middle

Subject teachers, labs, coding and a house system. Students learn to plan work and ask better questions.

Secondary

Board-aligned teaching for Classes IX–X with periodic tests, practicals and structured revision.

Senior Secondary

Science, Commerce and Humanities streams (as offered), with career talks and pre-board practice.

Teaching methodology

Experiential learning

Labs, models and field tasks so ideas are practised, not only copied.

Smart classes

Interactive boards and curated digital lessons in every classroom.

Project-based learning

Term projects that connect subjects and end in a student exhibition.

Doubt-clearing sessions

Weekly slots to ask, retry and leave the topic clearer than they found it.

Collaborative work

Pair and group tasks that build explanation skills and fairness.

Values in the timetable

Assemblies and circle time that treat courtesy as a practised habit.
