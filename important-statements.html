RKris Academy respects the privacy of parents, students and visitors. This policy explains what we collect through the website and why.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or admission form we may collect the student’s name, date of birth, class applied for, parent or guardian name, phone, email, address and your message. Career applications may include a CV sent by email.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, process admission interest, and contact you about school-related matters you asked about. We do not sell personal data.

WhatsApp and email

If you submit a form, we may open a WhatsApp message or send email so the office can reply. Please do not include more personal detail than needed.

Photographs

Campus photographs on this site may include students. They are used to show school life. If you wish a photograph of your child to be removed, write to us and we will take it down where we reasonably can.

Your requests

To access, correct or delete information you sent through this website, email info@rkrisacademy.edu.in with the subject line “Privacy request — RKris Academy”.
